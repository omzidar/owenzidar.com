MPA courses

SPI 511c: Microeconomic Analysis for Policymakers (Advanced)
Princeton · Fall 2021, Fall 2023

An introduction to microeconomic policy analysis at an advanced level. The course explores several policy areas and focuses on applied economic and empirical methods to show how economists combine theory and data to evaluate policies.

Syllabus
SPI 593i: The Future of Fiscal Policy: American Economic Policy Debates in the 21st Century
Princeton · 2017–2022

Evaluates fiscal policies that governments will likely consider in the 21st century, from both empirical and theoretical perspectives, in light of technological change, automation, and geographic sorting.

SPI 593j: Combining Theory and Data to Evaluate Policy
Princeton · Fall 2019, Spring 2021

Policy debates such as raising top taxes, expanding the EITC, and reforming health insurance require assessing how policy changes affect economic activity, government budgets, and the distribution of resources. The course evaluates the Tax Cuts and Jobs Act, EITC expansion, local economic development policies, and health insurance reforms.
