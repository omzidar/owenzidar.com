The Everywhere Millionaire

Who Is Really Rich in America and How They Got There

Owen Zidar and Eric Zwick · Henry Holt · September 2026

This book combines statistics and stories on ordinary Americans who have gotten extraordinarily wealthy running private businesses.

Praise

“A Whitmanian portrait of capitalism.” “Brilliant…Vivid…. Zidar and Zwick discovered what comes close to being a new class in America.”
—The New Yorker
“The Everywhere Millionaire tells a new story about America’s rich and provides deep insight into the US economy.”
—Financial Times
“Through a painstaking examination of tax records and a series of entertaining anecdotal portraits, Zidar and Zwick present a very different profile of the 21st-century American capitalist.”
—The New York Times
“This book will be of great interest to those who think the rich have it rigged…. The book will appeal even more to those who hope to join their ranks.”
—The Economist
“This book is a revelation!… A vitally important book that will change what we think and which has a shot at changing what we do.”
—Angus Deaton, Nobel Prize–winning economist
“A brilliant and deep analysis of what really matters in modern America—who is rich and why.”
—Simon Johnson, Nobel Prize–winning economist
